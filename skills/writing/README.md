# Writing

[write-evidence-driven-blog](write-evidence-driven-blog/SKILL.md) converts
technical experience into original, evidence-driven Chinese articles.

[explaining-in-plain-language](explaining-in-plain-language/SKILL.md) turns a
tangled technical situation into a short plain-language explanation with a
working metaphor, for notes, questions, or telling someone what happened. Keep
scale separate: this skill stays at a two-minute read, while the blog skill
starts when the material is long enough to deserve an article.

Drafting and revising do not authorize publication. Preserve the distinction
between the user's decisions and the agent's implementation or investigation.
